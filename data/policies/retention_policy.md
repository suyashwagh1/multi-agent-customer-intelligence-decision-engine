# Retention Policy

## Retention Discount Eligibility
Retention discounts may be offered to customers who express clear intent to
cancel, stop ordering, or switch to a competitor, subject to a maximum
discount of 20% per customer. This cap applies per customer account, not
per conversation or per order.

## Discount Sizing Guidance
Retention discounts should be sized based on the customer's risk profile,
not offered at the maximum by default:
- High lifetime value + high churn risk: up to 20% discount, or an
  equivalent account credit.
- High lifetime value + low-to-medium churn risk: 5-10% is typically
  sufficient, since the retention risk itself is lower.
- Low lifetime value + high churn risk: a discount is appropriate but
  should generally stay in the 10-15% range given the smaller account
  value at stake.
- New customers with fewer than 2 orders: retention discounts should not
  be applied automatically; these cases should be escalated, since churn
  signals are unreliable with limited order history.

## One Discount Per Customer
Once a retention discount has been applied to a customer's account, a
second retention discount should not be applied automatically within a
12-month period. A repeat request within this window should be escalated
rather than resolved with another automatic discount.

## Retention Discounts Are Not a Substitute for Resolving the Underlying Issue
If a customer's stated reason for wanting to cancel is a specific
unresolved issue (a damaged item, a late order, an unresolved ticket),
that issue should be addressed directly first. A discount offered without
addressing the underlying complaint often does not actually retain the
customer and should not be treated as the primary response.

## Escalation Threshold
See Escalation Rules for cases where a retention conversation should be
handed to a human agent instead of receiving an automatic discount.
