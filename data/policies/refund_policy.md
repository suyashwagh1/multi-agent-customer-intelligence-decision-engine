# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery, provided the item
meets the eligibility requirements below. The 30-day window is calculated
from the delivery date shown in tracking, not the order date.

## Return Window and Refund Timing
Once a return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5-7 business days. Store credit refunds, when chosen
instead of the original payment method, are issued within 1-2 business days.

## Damaged or Defective Items
Items that arrive damaged or defective are eligible for a full refund
regardless of the standard 30-day window, provided the customer reports the
issue within 45 days of delivery. Photo evidence of the damage may be
requested before the refund is approved. Damaged-item refunds do not require
the item to be shipped back if the damage is visually confirmed.

## Wrong Item Received
If a customer receives an item different from what was ordered, they are
eligible for either a full refund or a free replacement shipment, at the
customer's choice. This applies regardless of the standard return window,
provided it is reported within 45 days of delivery.

## Late Delivery Exceptions
If an order arrives more than 14 days after the original estimated delivery
date, the customer is eligible for a partial refund of shipping costs even
if they choose to keep the item. This is independent of any product-level
refund and does not require a return.

## Final-Sale and Clearance Items
Items marked final-sale or clearance at checkout are not eligible for a
refund based on buyer's remorse or preference. However, final-sale items
that arrive damaged or defective are still covered under the Damaged or
Defective Items section above -- final-sale status does not waive quality
guarantees.

## Premium Customer Exceptions
Premium-tier customers receive an extended refund window of 45 days instead
of the standard 30 days, and are not subject to the final-sale restriction
for buyer's-remorse returns up to $100 in order value per calendar year.

## Refund Eligibility Summary
A refund request may be denied if: the item is outside all applicable
windows above, the item shows signs of use beyond inspection, or the item
is excluded under a specific category policy (see Return Policy for
category exclusions).
