# Return Policy

## General Return Window
Eligible products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be
in their original condition, unworn or unused where applicable, and include
original packaging where possible. A missing box or tag does not
automatically disqualify a return, but may reduce the refund amount at the
inspector's discretion for resellable-condition items.

## Category Exclusions
The following categories are excluded from standard returns unless the item
is damaged or defective on arrival (see Refund Policy):
- Perishable goods (food, plants, flowers)
- Personal care items that have been opened (cosmetics, undergarments)
- Digital downloads and gift cards
- Custom or personalized items

## Return Process
1. Customer initiates a return request through support or the order history
   page, specifying a reason.
2. A prepaid return label is issued for eligible returns; the customer is
   not responsible for return shipping costs on standard returns.
3. Once the returned item is received at the warehouse, inspection typically
   takes 2-3 business days.
4. Upon passing inspection, the refund or exchange is processed per the
   Refund Policy.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color of the same product are treated as
a return plus a new order, but do not require the customer to wait for the
refund to complete before the replacement ships -- replacement shipments go
out as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier, not after inspection.

## Return Window Extensions
Items purchased as gifts, when the gift recipient (not the purchaser) is
requesting the return, receive a return window measured from the delivery
date, not the purchase date, and default to a 45-day window to account for
delayed gift-opening.
