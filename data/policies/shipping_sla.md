# Shipping SLA

## Standard Shipping
Standard orders are normally delivered within 5-7 business days after the
order ships (not after the order is placed -- processing time is separate,
see below). Business days exclude weekends and major holidays.

## Processing Time Before Shipment
Orders typically ship within 1-2 business days of being placed. During
high-volume periods (e.g. major sales events), processing time may extend
to 3 business days. This processing window is separate from and precedes
the shipping SLA above.

## Expedited Shipping
Expedited shipping guarantees delivery within 2 business days of shipment.
If an expedited order does not arrive within this window, the customer is
eligible for a full refund of the expedited shipping fee, regardless of
whether the product itself is returned.

## Delayed Delivery Threshold
An order is considered "delayed" if it has not arrived within 3 business
days past the applicable SLA window (standard or expedited). Delayed orders
should be flagged for the Order Inquiry workflow; if delayed further, see
the Late Delivery Exceptions section of the Refund Policy.

## Lost Package Determination
If a package shows no tracking movement for 7 or more consecutive days
after shipment, it may be classified as lost in transit. Lost packages are
eligible for a full reshipment or refund at the customer's choice, without
requiring the customer to wait out the full delivery window.

## International Shipping
International orders are not covered by the standard 5-7 business day SLA.
International delivery estimates range from 7-21 business days depending on
destination and customs processing, and are provided at checkout on a
per-order basis.
